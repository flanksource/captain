## Space and size

Keep clear space equal to the central band's thickness: **40 units on the 256-unit symbol grid**, measured outside the visible artwork. Scale that distance with the logo. Built-in canvas padding counts towards this space; add the balance in the surrounding layout. Use the existing proportions and spacing within each lockup.

Minimum screen sizes: **horizontal 160 px wide; stacked 160 px wide; wordmark 128 px wide; small symbol 16 px square; app tile 32 px square**. Use the dedicated small drawing below 48 px. Print starting points are 35 mm horizontal, 30 mm stacked, and 6 mm symbol; inspect a physical proof before production.

## Typography and handling

The logo requires no installed font. Supporting text uses the platform's system sans-serif stack; monospace is reserved for commands and code. No font files are distributed.

Preserve the mark's orientation, silhouette, three separate bands, and lockup proportions. Use the supplied reverse and small-size drawings. Keep artwork flat and leave clear space free of text or decoration. All SVG masters remain editable vector outlines.

Masters and handover notes live beside this guide. Public trademark clearance remains outside the supplied artwork checks.
